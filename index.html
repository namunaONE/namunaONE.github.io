04 · The method

Verified, not asserted.

Every figure on this page passes the same pipeline. Sources in, gates on, gaps declared — and the gaps travel with the data.

  1. 01

    Inventory

    Five independent rosters merged and diffed. Duplicates and roster defects logged, never smoothed over.

  2. 02

    Verify

    CBS/NSO codes cross-checked against a postal roster: 548 of 753 doubly attested, 205 single-source, 0 invented.

  3. 03

    Adjudicate

    72 conflicts, 72 resolved — each with a written record of which source won, and why.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Byte-stable generation and CI drift checks. Confidence tiers are exposed in the API, not buried in a spreadsheet.

05 · About

About Namuna One

namuna नमूना
noun · Nepali · "na-MU-naa"
  1. A sample; a specimen kept for comparison.
  2. A pattern; an example worth copying.
  3. The reference you check everything else against.

Why the name? A नमूना is the sample you show to prove the work is real. Namuna One is that sample, published — the datasets, the packages, the sites. Sharing is caring — so the samples are open.

  1. 01Verified over invented. Every figure traces to a source; every gap is declared, never papered over.
  2. 02Offline-first. Data ships inside the package. No network, no excuses, no per-seat fees.
  3. 03Bilingual, always. Devanagari and Latin side by side — names taken from source rosters, never transliterated or guessed.
  4. 04Free and open. MIT for code; the data keeps its sources' licenses (CC-BY-4.0, CC0) with full attribution.
  5. 05Built in Nepal. Maintained by namunaONE under HyaMuji and Taire Bhai — not affiliated with the Government of Nepal.
